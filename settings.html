📖 About

Twitch Auto Claimer automatically claims channel point rewards when they appear. Supports per-channel tracking with beautiful dark theme.

Twitch Auto Claimer 自動領取 Twitch 頻道點數獎勵，支援每頻道獨立統計，深色狐狸主題。

💡 How It Works

1. Install the extension
2. Make sure Auto-claim is enabled
3. Visit Twitch channels
4. Rewards are claimed automatically!

1. 安裝擴充功能
2. 確保自動領取已開啟
3. 造訪 Twitch 頻道
4. 獎勵會自動領取！

🔒 Privacy

This extension does not collect any personal data. All data is stored locally in your browser. No tracking, no analytics, no servers.

此擴充功能不會收集任何個人資料。所有資料都儲存在瀏覽器本地，無追蹤、無分析、無伺服器。
